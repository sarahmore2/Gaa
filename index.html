Why GAA matters

GAA sports are for everyone — learn the rules, practice safe training, and have fun with interactive games.

Basic Rules (short)

  • Teams: Usually 15 players on each team in Gaelic football and hurling.
  • Scoring: Kick or strike the ball between goal posts — over the bar = 1 point, under the bar (into the net) = 3 points.
  • Ball handling: You can carry, bounce, and kick or hand-pass the ball. There are limits (learn details below).
  • Fair play: Respect opponents, follow the referee's calls, and play safely.

This is a simplified summary — encourage younger readers to ask coaches for full guidance.

Healthy Diet Suggestions

Simple, balanced tips for players of all ages:

  • Breakfast: Oats, fruit, and yoghurt for good slow energy.
  • Lunch: Lean protein (chicken, beans), whole grains and vegetables.
  • Snacks: Bananas, nuts, or a small sandwich before training.
  • Hydration: Drink water before, during, and after activity.

Warm-up & Stretches

  1. Light jog for 5 minutes to raise heart rate.
  2. Leg swings — 10 each side (dynamic stretch).
  3. Hamstring stretch — hold 20 seconds each leg.
  4. Hip circles and ankle rolls to loosen joints.

Always start gently and stop if something hurts. Coaches should supervise children.
